## beacon

- system `beacon` -- A Nostr relay in pure Common Lisp: an append-only event log, in-memory unboxed indexes, a parallel signature-verify pool, a single group-commit writer, and indexed live fanout.  
  depends on: `secp256k1-fast`, `json-simple`, `sb-bsd-sockets`, `sb-posix`
